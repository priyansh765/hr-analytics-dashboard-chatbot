COMPANY HR POLICY DOCUMENT

1. LEAVE POLICY
Employees are entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. 
Sick leave is provided separately, up to 12 days per year, and requires a 
medical certificate if taken for more than 2 consecutive days. Leave requests 
must be submitted through the HR portal at least 3 days in advance, except 
for emergency or sick leave.

2. NOTICE PERIOD
The standard notice period for resignation is 60 days for employees at the 
Manager level and above, and 30 days for employees below Manager level. 
During the notice period, employees are expected to complete a proper 
handover of their responsibilities.

3. WORKING HOURS
Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday, with a 
1-hour lunch break. Employees working in shifts follow a separate roster 
schedule communicated by their department head.

4. OVERTIME POLICY
Employees who work beyond standard hours due to project deadlines may be 
eligible for overtime compensation, subject to manager approval. Overtime 
is compensated at 1.5x the regular hourly rate for non-exempt employees.

5. WORK FROM HOME POLICY
Employees may request up to 2 days of work-from-home per week, subject to 
manager approval and role suitability. Fully remote arrangements require 
approval from the department head and HR.

6. PERFORMANCE REVIEW
Performance reviews are conducted twice a year, in June and December. 
Reviews are based on goal completion, peer feedback, and manager assessment. 
Promotions and salary increments are primarily decided during the December 
review cycle.

7. EMPLOYEE BENEFITS
All full-time employees are eligible for health insurance covering self, 
spouse, and up to 2 children. The company also provides a provident fund 
contribution matching 12% of the basic salary, and an annual wellness 
allowance of INR 10,000.

8. ATTRITION AND EXIT PROCESS
Employees who wish to resign must submit a formal resignation letter to 
their manager and HR. An exit interview is mandatory before the final 
settlement. Full and final settlement is processed within 45 days of the 
last working day.

9. CODE OF CONDUCT
Employees are expected to maintain professionalism, avoid conflicts of 
interest, and report any workplace harassment immediately to HR. Violation 
of the code of conduct may lead to disciplinary action, including 
termination in severe cases.

10. TRAINING AND DEVELOPMENT
The company provides an annual learning budget of INR 15,000 per employee 
for certifications, courses, or conferences related to their role. Internal 
training sessions are conducted quarterly.
